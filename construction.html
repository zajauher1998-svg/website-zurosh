Our Delivery Process

A structured four-stage process, managed by a single project team from initial design to handover.

1 · Planning & Design

Client brief, architectural and structural design, bill of quantities and budget.

2 · Approvals

Building plan approval from the relevant development authority.

3 · Construction

Structural, MEP and finishing works under continuous site supervision and quality control.

4 · Handover

Snagging, final inspection and handover with complete documentation.
